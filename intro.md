# AirGuard nRF54L15 DK Find My Scanner

AirGuard nRF54L15 DK turns a Nordic development board into a compact embedded scanner for nearby AirTag-class Find My Bluetooth advertisements. The project targets situations where a phone app is not enough: lab demonstrations, fixed-location monitoring, embedded privacy experiments, and course projects that need a reproducible BLE sensing platform. The firmware runs directly on the nRF54L15 DK, performs passive BLE scans every three minutes, filters Apple manufacturer data for AirTag-class payloads, and prints concise serial reports with RSSI, battery status, observation history, and possible repeated-presence warnings. In the current build, detections appear after a 15 second scan window, and the repeated-presence heuristic flags devices seen in at least three reports over ten minutes. The result is an offline, inspectable baseline for studying tracker detection on resource-constrained hardware without depending on a phone, cloud service, or private Find My network access.
